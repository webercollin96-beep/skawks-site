Who we are

We are a compact Iowa team fueled by a love of great games. Because we are players at heart, we obsess over the fine details of everything we create, and yes, we actually read and reply to our own emails.

What we make

Our focus is on original titles that grip you the second you start playing. We chase striking atmospheric experiences, compelling mechanical loops, and completely novel ideas that refuse to blend into the crowd.

Our promise

We don't do clones, and we don't cut corners. Every title carrying our name is an experience we are genuinely proud to play ourselves.

Player survey

Want to help us improve our game?

We're a small studio, and the people who play our games shape what we make next. After you play, tell us what worked and what didn't in a short survey:

  • How fun it was, from 1 to 5 stars
  • What you liked, and what you'd change
  • Anything confusing, or too hard or too easy
  • Whether you'd want more Acts

We read every answer, and we use them to fix problems and tune the game before and after launch.

The survey opens with our first playtest. Want in early? Email us with the subject "Playtest" and we'll send it to you.
