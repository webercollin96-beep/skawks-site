A Nomad's Duality: Ashbound

Guide a soul undone by family tragedy, and carve a path through the relentless agony that haunts them.

Faith versus Despair

This dark fantasy action RPG chronicles a pious character whose devotion is tested by absolute tragedy. Stripped of their spouse and two children, this broken soul roams from town to town, running blindly from the grief that stalks them, until a fortune teller presents them with a mystical lantern.

This treacherous quest unfolds across seven distinct Acts modeled after the stages of loss, demanding the wanderer endure shock, denial, and depression before reaching acceptance. In every chapter, they breach a new territory, overthrow its cruel overlord, and retrieve a lost relic of their family.

The chronicle begins in the suffocating shadows of the Weeping Woods, now being forged.

Blood & Iron

The Art of Slaughter

Guard Your Reserves

Every swinging strike, sudden dodge, and desperate block depletes your stamina. Exhausting your energy leaves you entirely defenseless, making tactical patience a matter of life and death.

Seize the Stagger

Watch your foes closely. When an enemy's weapon flashes a brilliant white, time your block perfectly to shatter their stance. This leaves them staggered, opening a brief window to unleash devastating retaliation.

Evade the Unblockable

When a foe glows an ominous red, discard all thoughts of blocking. These strikes pierce through any shield, so a perfectly timed dodge is your only salvation.

Ruthless Defenses

Malicious foes dodge your strikes, guard against careless blows, and brutally punish anyone who mindlessly spams attacks. Monstrous bosses grow increasingly lethal and aggressive as their health bleeds away.

Armed for the Void

Carve Your Legend

Six Nomads

Choose your path from six distinct disciplines: Lightwarden, Ranger, Warrior, Monk, Witch, or Nightblade. Each wields specialized armaments and commands its own signature skills and lethal abilities.

The Sevenfold Infusion

Channel the power of your ascension into a sprawling Skill Tree with points earned from leveling up. Beyond your core class abilities, the tree forks into seven primal elemental paths (fire, ice, lightning, earth, water, holy, and shadow), allowing you to imbue weapons, armor, and fresh talents with mystical properties.

Claim Your Spoils. Forge Your Fate.

Scour the ruined world for rare armaments boasting chaotic, random traits. Claim these artifacts, augment them with mystical socketed gems, and bend them to your will. When the road ahead demands greater strength, take your trusted blade to the anvil. Use rare crafting materials to hone your gear, keeping your favorite weapons lethal against the rising tide of darkness.

Swear Desperate Alliances

You do not have to walk the abyss alone. Enlist the services of a sellsword, a bowyer, a hedge witch, or a wandering scholar, each with their own skills.

The Perilous Path

Endure, Advance, or Perish

The Weight of the Dark

The longer you tread through the oppressive shadows without respite, the higher your Agony builds, gradually draining your strength. Seek refuge at Vigil Lanterns to channel your experience, level up, and ease your Agony.

Contracts of the Board

Every settlement hosts a bounty board overflowing with perilous side quests. Hunt down designated abominations and fulfill high-risk contracts to secure gold, rare equipment, and bonus experience.

Hidden Horrors

Monstrous, elite foes lie hidden far off the beaten path for brave souls willing to explore. Slaying these optional terrors yields far greater loot and significant experience.

Confront the True Terror

Once an Act is conquered, test your resolve by replaying it in Nightmare mode. This unforgiving realm features far deadlier enemies, but rewards survivors with exceptionally rare loot and massive experience.

Free to play

A Fair Fight

The Pilgrim's Ascent

Earn a steady stream of rewards simply by playing on the free tier, or ascend to the premium tier of the Pilgrimage Pass to maximize your rewards.

Vanities of the Lost

Coming soon: purely aesthetic cloaks and armor skins to customize your wanderer's appearance. They offer no advantages and have zero impact on combat stats.

The Horizon of Souls

Our post-launch roadmap includes online features. Look forward to testing your steel in ranked duels, founding powerful clans, and tackling perilous co-op raids.
